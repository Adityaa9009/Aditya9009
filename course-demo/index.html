Learning objectives

01 — RecognizeIdentify common social-engineering signals in messages and links.
02 — DecideChoose the safest next action under realistic workplace pressure.
03 — ReportKnow what to do when a suspicious message reaches your inbox.

Scenario: The urgent invoice

You receive this message ten minutes before a client call. It asks you to review an invoice immediately.

From: finance-team@acme-billing.co   •   Subject: URGENT — Invoice approval required
Hi Aditya,

We noticed an overdue invoice. Please verify the attached payment details before 5:00 PM to avoid service interruption.

Review invoice securely

Thanks,
Accounts Payable

Knowledge check

Which signal is the strongest reason to slow down and verify the message?

Ready to apply.

The learner has demonstrated the core behavior: pause, verify independently, and use a trusted reporting path rather than trusting the message itself.
